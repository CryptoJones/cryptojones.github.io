The United Nations Security Council held a meeting about artificial
intelligence and world safety. The video was posted on September 23, 2026.
France ran the meeting. Four people from the AI world spoke first: Yoshua
Bengio, Sam Altman of OpenAI, Dario Amodei of Anthropic, and Clément Delangue
of Hugging Face. Then all 15 member countries gave a statement.

> **Security Council**: the part of the UN in charge of keeping peace between
> countries. It has 15 member countries. France held the chair this month.

I watched this for a class on AI and society, and I wanted a transcript I could
read and search. YouTube's captions run every word together, with no names and
no times. So I rebuilt it. Each speaker has a heading. Each paragraph has a time
stamp. Click a time stamp to open the video at that moment.

A few things to know before you read:

- The words come from YouTube's automatic captions. This is not an official UN
  record.
- The captions get names wrong a lot, like "Alman" for Altman and "Amade" for
  Amodei. The headings use the right spellings.
- When the chair did not say a speaker's name, the heading shows only the country.
- Chair remarks are in italics. The chair mostly thanks one speaker and calls the
  next one.
