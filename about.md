I'm **Aaron K. Clark** — **CryptoJones** online. *Passionate, dedicated, curious:*
a lifelong student and sometimes a teacher. Professionally my focus has been
**Information Technology, Software Engineering, and Cybersecurity**.

Right now I'm working through the Rochester Institute of Technology's Advanced
Graduate Certificate in Cybersecurity (via edX). Along the way I've picked up
graduate certificates in IT Project Management (Colorado State University) and
Computer Science Foundations (Kennesaw State University), plus a Bachelor's in
Computer and Information Science and an Undergraduate Certificate in Unix System
Administration from the University of Maryland University College.

Originally from Houston, Texas, I've lived in Nebraska since 2008 — after five
years in the Marines. When I'm away from a keyboard you'll find me traveling,
working on carpentry projects in the shop, keeping my home network alive, or
running a session of **Alternity (1998)**, the greatest tabletop RPG ever made.

### This blog

Write-ups of things I've actually built or broken: homelab plumbing, Tailscale
and UniFi networking, local LLM inference, the occasional Windows-server war
story, and security odds and ends. Real commands, real gotchas, and
generic-enough details that you can paste them into your own setup — the guide
I'd have wanted to find myself at 2 a.m.
