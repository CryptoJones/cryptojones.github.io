Context for the numbers

  • A 35B mixture of experts model with roughly 3B active parameters reaches about 62 tok/s on this same pair of cards. This dense 27B reaches 12. That is the dense penalty, and it is about five times.
  • Prompt processing stays healthy at 198 tok/s because that work is limited by compute, not by memory bandwidth. Generation is the opposite.
  • A different 27B model squeezed down to roughly 2 bits runs at about 10 tok/s on a 16 GB Apple M2 Pro. So this setup is a little faster while keeping a far better quantization.
  • No 4 bit build of a dense 27B fits inside 16 GB. The 4 bit file is 17.11 GB. Two cards, or one big card, is the honest requirement.

Turning the thinking off, and why it can backfire

This model reasons before it answers. The reasoning comes back in its own field, separate from the reply. You can switch it off per request. The obvious guess is that switching it off always makes things faster. That guess is wrong.

On the two easy tasks, switching thinking off is a big win. On the task that actually needed reasoning it made things worse. The model did not stop reasoning. It moved the reasoning into the visible answer, used 60 percent more tokens, and took longer.

Look at the answer length on the last row. With thinking on, the reply was 6 characters, just the number asked for, because the working happened in the reasoning field. With thinking off, the reply was 6,033 characters of working out loud.

That is the part worth knowing if you care about context. The reasoning field is usually dropped by the client when it builds the next turn of a conversation. The answer field is not. So on reasoning heavy work, turning thinking off can grow a conversation faster rather than slower, which is the opposite of what people expect.

Rule of thumb: turn it off for lookups, formatting, and short creative output. Leave it on for anything with steps in it.

Notes

  • The model reports its architecture as qwen35, so a llama.cpp build from June 2026 loaded it with no source changes.
  • It is a reasoning model. Replies come back with a separate reasoning block before the answer, so give it a generous token budget or the reply comes back empty.
  • It also ships a vision tower and a separate projector file, which was not exercised here.
